# Official Regional Site Map

This document maps official Pokémon Sword / Shield web surfaces by **region and language**. It exists to prevent a single English-language site from being treated as the complete non-Japanese release corpus.

The Japanese release remains the comparison baseline. Regional websites are compared against it for terminology, content selection, publication timing, products, promotions, competitions, ratings, and other presentation or service differences. A regional website does **not** by itself prove a distinct game binary.

## Region-selector evidence

The official regional selector exposed by the Sword/Shield website lists at least the following regions: América Latina, Australia/New Zealand, België, Belgique, Canada (EN), Canada (FR), Deutschland, España, France, Italia, Nederland, Portugal, Россия, UK/Ireland, and United States.

These are treated as separate official publication surfaces until comparison demonstrates that two routes are textually or structurally identical.

## High-value regional comparison pages already identified

### Terminology changes

The official English site contains a dedicated **Pokémon Sword and Pokémon Shield Terms** page explaining that several established translated terms were revised to better reflect the developers' original meaning and technical/graphical changes. This is a first-class localization source and must be compared against Japanese terminology and every available localized equivalent.

Confirmed examples:

- US: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay/glossary-terms/
- UK/Ireland: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-gb/gameplay/glossary-terms/

### Version-exclusive presentation

Confirmed localized version-exclusive pages include:

- France: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/fr-fr/gameplay/version-exclusives/
- Germany: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/de-de/gameplay/version-exclusives/
- Italy: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/it-it/gameplay/version-exclusives/

These pages are useful not only for gameplay data but also for official localized Pokémon, Gym Leader, form and version terminology.

### Products and Expansion Pass

Confirmed official product/Expansion Pass surfaces include:

- US product page: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-us/buy-now/
- UK/Ireland Expansion Pass root: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-gb/expansionpass/
- UK/Ireland Expansion Pass announcement: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-gb/expansionpass/announcement/
- Italy product page: https://swordshield.pokemon.com/it-it/buy-now/

## Required enumeration fields

Every regional page record should eventually contain:

- region;
- language;
- URL;
- localized page title;
- Japanese baseline counterpart, if one exists;
- publication/update date if exposed;
- category;
- content present only in this region/language;
- terminology differences;
- product/ratings/competition/distribution relevance;
- live/redirected/removed/archive state;
- verification and cross-check status.
