# Japanese Competition and Online-Service Ledger

This ledger tracks Japanese official Sword/Shield material for Battle Stadium, Ranked Battles, Casual Battles, official Internet Competitions, Friendly Competitions, Live Competitions, LAN play, participation rewards, competition-specific bugs and the final service-update state.

It is intentionally cross-linked with distribution and bug research because some competitions distribute Pokémon/items and some official notices exist only because a competition exposed a software/service defect.

## System baseline

### Battle Stadium

Official Japanese Sword/Shield documentation defines Battle Stadium as the online hub for:

- Ranked Battles;
- Casual Battles;
- Internet Competitions;
- Rental Teams.

Nintendo Switch Online is required for Battle Stadium Internet functionality. Pokémon Global Link membership is not required.

Source: https://www.pokemon.co.jp/ex/sword_shield/howtoplay/190816_01.html

### Competition modes and LAN play

The official competition guide documents:

- official Internet Competition registration and participation;
- Friendly Competition participation and creation;
- Live Competition participation, hosting and spectating;
- LAN play setup and workflow.

The in-game `VS` function becomes available after receiving the Dynamax Band.

Source: https://www.pokemon.co.jp/ex/sword_shield/howtoplay/191108_vs.html

## 2020 International Challenge February bug

An official notice documents a competition-specific defect:

- if the same Nintendo Account registered Battle Teams from both Sword and Shield, the version registered first could fail to match with opponents;
- the recommended workaround was to complete play in one version before locking the Battle Team in the other;
- the normal per-day match restriction was removed for the event and a maximum of 45 total matches was displayed/allowed across the three-day competition;
- the notice stated that the cause was under investigation and a future update fix was planned.

Source: https://www.pokemon.co.jp/info/2020/02/200221_at01.html

This record belongs in both competition history and the future bug/fix ledger. The exact game update in which the underlying defect was fixed still requires confirmation.

## PJCS2020 cancellation / replacement chain

The official 2020 cancellation notice records that Pokémon Japan Championships 2020 was cancelled due to COVID-19. Qualification rights from International Challenge events were affected, while International Challenge May still proceeded. A later official notice established the Pokémon Japan No.1 Deciding Battle 2020 as the replacement competitive pathway and reassigned relevant qualification rights.

Sources:

- https://www.pokemon.co.jp/info/2020/04/200422_at01.html
- https://www.pokemon.co.jp/info/2020/06/200612_at02.html

This chronology must be preserved rather than presenting the replacement competition as if it had always been the planned 2020 structure.

## PJCS2022 and distribution linkage

The PJCS2022 official page identifies four qualifying Internet Competitions:

1. 2022 International Challenge February;
2. 2022 International Challenge March;
3. 2022 International Challenge April;
4. PJCS2022 Qualifier in May.

The first three also supplied participation gifts:

- February — shiny Galarian Articuno;
- March — shiny Galarian Zapdos;
- April — shiny Galarian Moltres.

The umbrella rules use Sword/Shield and Series 12 regulations and define age divisions, permitted Pokémon, restricted-Pokémon limits, time controls and participation conditions. Individual event notices and the precise gift/Wonder Card records still require separate enumeration.

Source: https://www.pokemon.co.jp/info/2022/01/220114_e01.html

## Final service state

The 2022-10-14 official notice establishes the later Sword/Shield online-service state:

- Wild Area News receives no planned updates after the 2022-11-01 update;
- Ranked Battles receive no planned season updates after the November 2022 Season 36 / Series 14 state;
- that continuing season has no monthly final ranking/reward and is not reflected as normal ranked performance in mobile HOME;
- no further official Internet Competitions are planned;
- Friendly Competitions remain available, but HOME display coverage stops for competitions after 2022-11-30;
- Y-Comm trading, Surprise Trade, card exchange and link battles continue;
- online Pokémon Camp continues.

Source: https://www.pokemon.co.jp/info/2022/10/221014_at02.html
