# Japanese Distribution Source Ledger

This ledger tracks Japanese official distribution evidence for **Pokémon Sword / Shield**. It deliberately includes more than Pokémon gifts: item serial codes, clothing, Dynamax Crystals, HOME-linked rewards, local-store gifts, movie tie-ins and other Mystery Gift-compatible campaigns all belong in the distribution corpus.

The dedicated Sword/Shield news index is only one discovery surface. General `pokemon.co.jp/info/`, Pokémon Center notices, movie pages, campaign microsites, tournament broadcasts and Pokémon HOME notices must also be searched.

## Distribution classes that must be exhaustively enumerated

The final Japanese corpus must separately cover:

- Internet Mystery Gift distributions (`via Internet`);
- shared/password Mystery Gifts (`あいことば`);
- unique serial-code distributions;
- Pokémon Center / Pokémon Store local campaigns;
- retailer campaigns;
- magazine/book codes;
- movie/anime tie-in distributions;
- competition-stream or event passwords;
- Battle Stadium / tournament participation rewards;
- Wild Area / Max Raid global-goal rewards;
- Pokémon HOME-linked gifts and rewards;
- save-data linkage gifts;
- product-bundle item/clothing codes;
- Dynamax Crystal serial codes;
- item-only distributions;
- clothing-only distributions;
- Pokémon distributions, including version-dependent redemption;
- distributions whose original destination is removed or redirected.

## Required final record fields

Each distribution should eventually record, where publicly verifiable:

- official Japanese campaign name and source URL;
- announcement date;
- distribution start/end and redemption expiry separately;
- delivery mechanism;
- eligible game/version and Expansion Pass requirement;
- region and language restrictions;
- Pokémon species/form, level, OT/TID/SID, ball, moves, ability, nature, held item, ribbons/marks and other fixed/random attributes where publicly evidenced;
- item/clothing/Dynamax Crystal contents;
- per-save / per-code / per-person limits;
- Internet/Nintendo Account/Nintendo Switch Online requirements;
- corresponding Wonder Card / WC8 / BCAT evidence when independently available;
- matching Project Pokémon or other archival record;
- corresponding regional distributions and differences;
- preservation state and verification level.

## Known incompleteness

This list is **not** the complete Japanese Sword/Shield distribution list. Password distributions announced during tournaments/streams, official competition gifts, anime serial/password gifts, Wild Area global rewards, HOME gifts, additional Dynamax Crystal campaigns, and campaign microsites still require systematic enumeration. Secondary event databases will be used as discovery indexes and then traced back to primary evidence whenever possible.
