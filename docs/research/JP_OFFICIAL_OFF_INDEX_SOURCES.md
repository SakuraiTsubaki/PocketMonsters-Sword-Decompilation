## Additional official discovery surfaces that must be exhausted

The Japanese official corpus is broader than `pokemon.co.jp/ex/sword_shield/`. The exhaustive survey must also enumerate and search:

- `pokemon.co.jp/info/YYYY/` general official news pages for every year relevant to Sword/Shield;
- Pokémon Center / Pokémon Store campaign and local-distribution notices;
- official tournament, championship and battle-event notices;
- movie and anime tie-in distributions that deliver data to Sword/Shield;
- retailer campaigns and serial-code distributions;
- Pokémon HOME notices affecting Sword/Shield transfer or gifts;
- Nintendo Japan product, support, eShop and update pages;
- Expansion Pass official microsite pages;
- official YouTube videos and descriptions linked from campaign/news pages;
- official PDFs, downloadable flyers, rule documents, type charts and event lists;
- pages removed or redirected from their original destination, using archival discovery when necessary.

## Deduplication rule

A page may appear both in the dedicated Sword/Shield index and in general `pokemon.co.jp/info/` search results. Such a page is **one factual source with multiple discovery paths**, not two independent confirmations. The manifest must therefore preserve discovery provenance without inflating the number of independent sources.
